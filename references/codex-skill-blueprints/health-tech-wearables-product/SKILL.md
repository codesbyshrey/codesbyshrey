# Health-Tech Wearables Product

## Use This Skill When

- Designing a wearable or health dashboard.
- Turning biometric data into user guidance.
- Reviewing AI coaching or behavior-change features.
- Writing product, portfolio, or resume material for health-tech.
- Assessing claims about sleep, recovery, strain, HRV, labs, gait, or movement.

## Core Workflow

1. Define the user and health outcome.
2. Identify the signal: wearable, lab, behavior, clinical, environment, or self-report.
3. Identify evidence quality and validation level.
4. Map the behavior-change loop.
5. Define what the user can safely do with the insight.
6. Add privacy, consent, and escalation boundaries.
7. Avoid diagnostic claims unless the product is validated and regulated for them.

## Product Loop

Capture data.
Add context.
Detect patterns.
Explain tradeoffs.
Recommend action.
Observe outcome.
Refine guidance.

## Review Checklist

- Is the metric understandable?
- Is the recommendation actionable?
- Is the confidence level clear?
- Is missing data handled?
- Does the product avoid overclaiming?
- Can users control sensitive context?
- Does the system distinguish wellness guidance from medical advice?
- Does it know when to stay quiet?
- Does it support clinician, coach, or caregiver review when appropriate?

## Evidence Checklist

- Sensor validity.
- Algorithm validation.
- Population diversity.
- Longitudinal adherence.
- Ground-truth comparison.
- Error and uncertainty.
- Privacy and consent.
- Outcome measurement.

## Done Criteria

- The product has a clear primary outcome.
- The data loop is explainable.
- The user receives fewer, better insights.
- Safety language is present.
- The design supports real behavior change instead of metric overload.

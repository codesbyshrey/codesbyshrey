### Portfolio Project Ideas

#### 1. FIFA 21 Players Dataset

Possible work:

- Use Python or Excel.
- Clean height and weight types.
- Separate joined columns by time.
- Clean and transform value, wage, and release clause columns into integer columns.
- Remove newline characters from hits column.
- Separate team and contract into separate columns.

#### 1.2 Data Science Job Postings on Glassdoor

Possible work:

- Convert salary into integers.
- Extract useful information from job descriptions.
- Remove numbers from company name columns.
- Create new features, such as state from location column.

#### 2.1 Stack Overflow Annual Developer Survey

Questions:

- What companies pay developers the most?
- How much does remote work matter?
- How does coding experience relate to pay?
- What methods do people use to code?
- How likely is someone to get a job with Microsoft?

#### 2.2 Personal YouTube Watch History

Tools:

- Python.
- BeautifulSoup.
- NLTK.

Questions:

- What are the most watched videos?
- What recurring topics or keywords appear in titles?
- What days or hours have the most watching?
- What comments or patterns appear around viewing behavior?

#### 3.1 London Bike Sharing Dataset

Questions:

- How does weather affect bike usage?
- What are total bike rides in specific time frames?
- Can a 2-week moving average or other moving average reveal trends?
- Can visuals become more dynamic and user friendly?

Tools:

- Tableau.
- Power BI.

#### 3.2 Personal Bank Statements

Questions:

- Are there specific days, weeks, or months with more spending?
- What recurring purchases and frequencies appear?
- Where are expense spikes?
- What outliers exist in spending patterns?

Tools:

- Power BI.
- Tableau.

#### 4.1 U.S. Traffic Accidents

Focus:

- Data engineering.
- ETL pipeline.
- Modeling.

Questions:

- What should be included in fact tables and dimensions?
- What is the best ETL pipeline?
- How should the pipeline handle dataset changes and variable size?

Tools:

- Python.
- Airflow for automation.

#### 4.2 Credit Card Approval Prediction

Focus:

- Machine learning.
- Data science.

Questions:

- How do you handle unbalanced data?
- How do you define whether a customer is good or bad?
- How do you build an ML model for prediction?

Tools:

- Python.
- scikit-learn.

## Interview Talking Points

### Why WHOOP Matters As A Product Signal

WHOOP is interesting because it is not only tracking metrics. It is using longitudinal biometric data, habit context, research validation, and AI guidance to create a behavior-change loop. That is the direction health-tech is moving: from dashboards to decision support.

### Why RAG Matters For Health AI

Health AI cannot rely on generic model knowledge alone. It needs trusted sources, retrieval traces, refusal behavior, permissions, and careful distinction between wellness guidance and clinical claims.

### Why Human Factors Matters More In AI

AI lowers the cost of generating interfaces and workflows, but it raises the value of understanding real users, cognitive load, clinical realities, accessibility, trust, and behavior change.

### Why FDE Fits

FDE work is about making AI work inside real organizations. The important work is mapping workflows, understanding source systems, connecting data, building prototypes, managing ambiguity, and turning field learning into product infrastructure.
